Kraal Curation grows out of Kraal, the theatre production by Jason Jacobs, the 2026 Standard Bank Young Artist for Theatre. From 12 to 18 December 2026, Kharkams in Namaqualand hosts a week of live arts under the theme Die Grond Onthou / The Land Remembers.

This is the first edition. Film and theatre producers, local managers and artists have come together to celebrate and invest in trans-local talent. The curatorial team is Woudine Dirkse, Adré Links, Gladwin Van Niekerk, Devon Delmar, Cleveland Hopp, Kwei Shun-Yu, Manfred Adams and Wade Van Zyl, with volunteers from the University of Cape Town, Nelson Mandela University and Kharkams.

Two theatre performances lead the program:

  • In Wag Van
  • A story-reading for young children

Workshops for children and elders, a work-in-process session where emerging creators get feedback from the curation community, and a Kraal Talent Competition for local artists complete the week.

The documentary Starting from Scratch looks at what it means to work in sacred spaces like Kharkams. It will be available for paid viewing in 2027.

On the evening before the Talent Competition, we close with a communal celebration of our cultures, with live DJs and visual art installations.

Kraal Curation is an invitation to listen to, and engage with, stories from Kharkams, presented in Kharkams.
