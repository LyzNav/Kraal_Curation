The Kraal Curation is an extension of the original theatre production titled Kraal by the 2026 Standard Bank Young Artist for Theatre, Jason Jacobs. It is a week of creative engagements with live arts, from 12 to 18 December 2026, under the theme Die Grond Onthou / The Land Remembers.

This is the first year. A multi-disciplinary arts event bringing together a committed team of film and theatre producers, local managers, and artists, focusing on both the celebration and investment in our trans-local talent. The curatorial team features Woudine Dirkse, Adré Links, Gladwin Van Niekerk, Devon Delmar, Cleveland Hopp, Kwei Shun-Yu, Manfred Adams and Wade Van Zyl, along with volunteers from the University of Cape Town and Kharkams.

The lineup includes the films:

  • Variations on a Theme
  • Al Wat Ek Weet
  • Notes from the Underground

In addition, a thoughtfully curated selection of short films from established African filmmakers will be showcased. A work-in-process segment offers emerging creators the chance to receive feedback directly from the curation community.

Three main theatre performances will engage audiences in a different language and spirit:

  • In Wag Van
  • Wonde
  • A story-reading for young children

A Kraal Talent Competition will spotlight emerging local artists, while a documentary titled Starting from Scratch will provide an insightful look into the experiences of working in sacred spaces like Kharkams. This doccie will be made available for paid viewing at a later stage in 2027.

The evening before the Talent Competition, the Kraal Curation will close in a communal celebration, highlighting our diverse cultures with live DJs and visual art installations; an event you won't want to miss! Kraal Curation is an invitation to listen and engage with stories from and presented in Kharkams, Namaqualand.
